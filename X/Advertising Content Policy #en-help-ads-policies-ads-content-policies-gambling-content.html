Gambling content

  1. Help Center
  2. X Ads Policies
  3. Ads content policies

This policy applies to monetization on X and X’s paid advertising products.

For more information about X’s paid advertising products, click here. 

For information about X’s other policies, click here.

What's the policy?

X prohibits the promotion of gambling content, except for campaigns targeting specified countries where it is allowed with restrictions as explained below.

Note: In certain cases listed below, advertisers are only permitted if they have prior authorization from X. Authorization starts with being in touch with a X account team. Instructions for contacting X are included in each case.

What products or services are subject to this policy?

This policy applies, but is not limited, to:

  • Online casinos and gambling, sports betting, and bingo
  • Physical, brick-and-mortar casino establishments
  • Online fantasy sports sites
  • Bonus codes, vouchers, and coupons redeemable for gambling content
  • E-books or websites facilitating or leading to online gambling-related content, including affiliates and other sites driving traffic to gambling
  • Gambling-related software, such as gambling software and poker odds calculators
  • Sites or services providing tips, odds, handicapping, sports picks, forecasts, or poker odds calculators
  • Scratch games
  • Lotteries
  • Games or apps played for money*

*For games involving NFTs or cryptocurrency tokens, please refer to our Financial Products & Services Policy.

What products or services are not subject to this policy?

This policy generally does not prohibit:

  • Hotels with a casino, where the focus is on the hotel
  • Gambling accessories, such as poker chips or pachinko machines
  • News and information about fantasy sports

How does this policy vary from country to country?

Advertisers targeting the countries below must obtain pre-approval to run Gambling Content ads by first getting certified.

Brick-and-mortar casinos

Advertisements for licensed brick-and-mortar casinos are globally prohibited, except when targeting the countries listed below:

  • Africa & Middle East: Ghana, Israel, Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda
  • Asia Pacific: Australia, New Zealand, South Korea    
  • Europe: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Croatia, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Macedonia, Netherlands, Norway, Romania, Serbia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, UK
  • Latin America: Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Honduras, Mexico, Panama, Peru, Uruguay
  • North America: Canada, US

National and State Licensed Lotteries

  • Lottery advertisements by government entities, or entities with exclusive responsibility for running a lottery directly on a government's behalf; and state-licensed lotteries, are permitted in selected markets.
  • Lottery advertisements must only target jurisdictions the advertiser is approved to operate/sell in.

The following additional requirements apply to gambling content advertisements targeting certain countries.

Argentina

  • The following gambling ads are allowed in Argentina, as long as the advertiser is licensed by approved gambling regulators.
    • Online gambling (Online casinos, Poker, Sports betting, Fantasy sports, Lotteries, and Race betting) 
  • Prior authorization from X is required.

Australia

  • The following are permitted with restrictions:

Austria

  • Online casino, online poker, sport betting, and brick-and-mortar casino advertisements provided by state-licensed entities in Austria are permitted. These advertisers are only allowed with prior authorization from X.

Belgium

  • Sites driving traffic to gambling operators in Belgium (except lottery aggregators) -- such as affiliates, tips or odds sites -- are permitted as long as they do not require a licence or other permission for their activities. These advertisers are only allowed with prior authorization from X. If an advertiser’s status changes in this regard, or the advertiser is the subject of any ruling of a legal, regulatory authority, or industry body pertinent to their activities, the advertiser must inform X immediately.

Bosnia and Herzegovina

Brazil

    • Poker and poker odds calculators, websites or services
    • Affiliates and aggregators
    • Free-to-play/-enter games with no prizes of value
  • Sites driving traffic to gambling operators in Brazil such as affiliates, tips or odds sites are permitted, as long as they hold the appropriate licence for their activities. Advertisements must adhere to Advertising Rules, including mandatory responsible gambling warnings and support links.

Croatia

These advertisers are only allowed with prior authorization from X.

Greece

Honduras

Hong Kong 

  • Lottery by state licensed entities is permitted with prior authorization from X.
  • Sports betting by state licensed entities is permitted with prior authorization from X.

India

  • Ads promoting esports with valid and subsisting certificate of registration from the Online Gaming Authority of India are permitted. 

  • Ads promoting online social games are permitted provided the advertiser holds a ‘Certificate of registration’ or ‘Determination Order’ as applicable, from the Online Gaming Authority of India.

  • Ads promoting online money games are prohibited across India, including if the game has been determined to be an online money game by the Online Gaming Authority and/or featured in the list of online money games maintained by it. Online money games can be both skill based games (for instance, fantasy sports, rummy, poker, horse race betting) or chance based games (for instance, Flush, Brag, Three card games (Teen Patti) and Bingo). 

  • Ads promoting gambling/betting are not permitted. 

  • All gaming advertisements must be compliant with the applicable law including the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 and Rules thereunder and also conform to ASCI’s Code for Self-Regulation. Advertisers are encouraged to review the India-specific Gambling T&Cs

Ireland

Israel

Italy

  • Games with a pay to play element with non-cash prizes worth JPY 1000 or less.

Kenya

What do advertisers need to know about this policy?

Review our guidelines and make sure you understand the requirements for your brand and business. You are responsible for all your promoted content on X. This includes complying with applicable laws and regulations regarding advertisements.

How to contact X

Please file a ticket with our support team for any of the following reasons:

  • You are an advertiser who is looking to promote gambling content in a specific market which requires prior authorization from X
  • You are an advertiser whose account or posts are under review, and you would like to check on the status or request an expedited review
  • You believe that your account or posts were incorrectly reviewed 

Learn more about blocking or dismissing ads by reading this article on “How to report X Ads”. To report an ad that is potentially violating these policies, use the Report an Ad form.

Using our Ads Policy, Rules, and our Terms of Service, we will examine reported violations and take appropriate action.

If you are interested in advertising on X and want to learn more, please visit business.x.com.
